About our club

A proud club representing the western suburbs of the Tamar Valley in Northern Tasmania for more than 70 years.

Our story

First established in 1948, Riverside Cricket Club is a proud club representing the western suburbs of the Tamar Valley in the Northern Tasmania Cricket Association competitions.

Known also as the Blues, Riverside CC have a number of teams in the local junior competitions starting from under 12s through to senior cricket, where multiple senior men's teams and a senior women's team represent the club.

Throughout the season, Riverside CC also hosts junior and Master Blaster days for kids who aren't quite old enough to take that next step into organised cricket.

Premierships & proud players

The Blues have won multiple A Grade men's and women's premierships, including a statewide T20 competition. Many players have represented our club at all levels.
